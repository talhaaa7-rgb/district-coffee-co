About us

One roaster. One oven. One neighborhood.

District Coffee Co. opened in 2019 with a simple idea: buy good coffee direct from the farms that grow it, roast it ourselves, and bake everything served alongside it from scratch, on-site, every morning. No trucked-in frozen pastry, no beans roasted a month ago on the other side of the country.

What we believe

Slow coffee, honest ingredients

We'd rather sell fewer things done well than a long menu done adequately. That's why the menu is short, the pastry case changes with the season, and every espresso is pulled to order — never sitting on a burner.
